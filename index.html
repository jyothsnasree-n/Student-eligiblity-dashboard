Contract Rules

A participant is Eligible for a certificate only when both conditions are satisfied:

  • • Covers all 3 activity categories: Learn, Build, and Share
  • • Earns a total point sum of at least 6 Points

Note: Repeated activity IDs for the same participant trigger a Duplicate participation validation error.
